Optional evidence and invocation paths appear only when present. Source
selection is metadata, not a copied tree or an atomic content fingerprint.
Selected excerpts and raster observations are integrity-bound retained evidence.
Native paths, credentials and raw provider streams remain private and are not
included in redacted exports.

Historical composite runs have no provider runtime stream or attempts.
Historical failed-run recovery inputs do not grant replay, child or compose
execution. Readers verify retained capture/blob identity and lineage; unavailable
historical evidence cannot be reconstructed from current files.

A committed publication is visible only when the epoch references its matching
immutable manifest and lineage-edge hashes. Provider-free P0/P1/P2 reconciliation
checks the journal and staged, installed, manifest, edge and epoch observations.
Cleanup protects active, incomplete, corrupt and required-ancestor state. Plans
and tombstones are removed after successful deletion.
